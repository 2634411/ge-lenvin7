Common questions

Does treatment hurt?

We treat under local anaesthesia. If you are afraid, sedation is possible. The doctor will explain how it works.

What if the price goes up during treatment?

The price in the written plan is final. If it rises not at your request, we pay the difference.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, up to 12 months with no interest. It takes a few minutes to set up.
