Landing page, company website or online store: what is the difference

Elena · 2026-11-16

These formats differ not in prestige but in what the visitor does. A landing page helps understand one offer, a company website helps choose a direction and check the contractor, and a store helps put products into an order.

Landing page: one offer on one page

It suits a detailed presentation of a service, a promotion or an event. For example, a workshop describes chair reupholstery: it shows its work, terms and how to get in touch. This is an illustrative situation, not a client case.

One page lets you explain the offer step by step and answer the main questions. But if you mix many different services with different terms, it is harder for the visitor to find what they need.

Company website: several sections

Useful when each direction needs its own page: equipment supply, installation, maintenance. General sections can hold company information, projects and contacts.

The number of employees does not determine the format. A small firm may need separate service pages, while a large company may need a dedicated landing page for a specific promotion.

Online store: products and the order path

In a store the customer selects items and quantities and places an order. A catalogue without a cart may be just a showcase; having a cart does not mean online payment is connected.

Availability, delivery, payment, catalogue changes and receiving orders need to be defined separately.
